# 商品封面生成方案

四张独立方形封面：Midjourney Standard、Cursor Pro、Claude Pro、ChatGPT Plus。以截图中的商品标题为准；首张的 Gemini 分类标签不用于封面。

统一规格：1024 × 1024，满版背景，中心主体周围保留至少 12% 安全边距，适合商品列表缩略图。现代简洁的数字服务商品封面，不含价格、月份、二维码、折扣、网址或水印。品牌名称与会员档位清晰可读；四张构图与字体层级一致。只制作素材，不改商品价格、分类、库存或支付数据。

## 1. Midjourney Standard

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square digital subscription product cover, one standalone image
Primary request: Create a premium square ecommerce cover for Midjourney Standard, in a consistent four-cover AI service collection.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed soft pearl background with a subtle violet and blue glow.
Subject: a refined luminous sailboat sculpture evoking creative exploration, centered and occupying about half the canvas.
Style/medium: polished restrained 3D editorial illustration, clean studio finish.
Composition/framing: generous whitespace; subject in upper center; exact title below in bold modern sans-serif, smaller membership tier underneath. Keep all elements inside a 12% safe margin.
Text (verbatim): "Midjourney" and "STANDARD"
Constraints: 1:1 square; same hierarchy as the Cursor, Claude and ChatGPT covers; no price, duration, discount, URL, QR code or watermark; do not add Gemini branding.
